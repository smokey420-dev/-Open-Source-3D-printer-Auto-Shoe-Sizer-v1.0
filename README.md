##👟 Open-Source Auto-Shoe Sizer v1.0Stop guessing your 3D printed shoe size. Start measuring.The biggest barrier to 3D printed footwear is the "fit." Most hobbyists simply scale an STL up or down by a percentage, which ruins wall thickness and sole geometry. This web-based tool uses Non-Uniform Lattice Scaling to match a shoe template to an actual 3D foot scan while preserving the structural integrity of the print.
##🚀 The MissionI created this tool because I saw too many people give up on 3D printing shoes after wasting kilos of filament on pairs that didn't fit. My goal is to make custom-fit footwear accessible to everyone with a printer.🛠 How it WorksUpload your Foot Scan (STL).Upload a Shoe Template (STL).Automatic Normalization: The app centers both models and calculates the bounding box of your foot.Precision Morphing: The app stretches the shoe's length and width to match your foot's exact mm dimensions + a comfort offset.Export: Download a one-of-a-kind STL sized specifically for you.

##📦 Installation & UsageThis is a Zero-Install web app.Download the AutomaticShoeSizer.html file.Open it in any modern desktop browser (Chrome/Edge recommended).No data is sent to a server—all processing happens locally on your computer for total privacy of your foot scans.
